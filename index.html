Pricing

Really one-time?

Yes. One payment, yours for life, including Pro features added later. No subscription, no renewal, nothing that expires.

Will free features move behind Pro?

No. The free app is the whole Task system, and it stays that way. Pro is only ever additive.

New phone?

Your purchase lives with your Apple Account. Reinstall, tap Restore Purchases, and it all comes back.

Why not a subscription?

Because an app like this should be something you own, not something you rent. Simple as that.

And honestly: if you never buy Pro, you're not missing anything you need. That's by design.

What you can add to a Task

Due Date

DUE DATERemove
Date23 Jun 202609:00Overdue

Pin a Task to a day, and to a time if the time matters. Today shows you what is due, and anything that slips turns Overdue so it cannot quietly disappear.

Leave the time off and it stays a day rather than a deadline.

Attachments

ATTACHMENTSRemove all
Photo2.2 MBView−
Scan.pdf480 KBView−
+Add File

Photos, PDFs and whatever else the Task needs to carry. Attach from your library, from Files, or straight from the camera.

Every attachment is copied into the app on your device. Nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload it to.

Location

LOCATIONRemove
King’s Cross StationEuston Rd, London N1 9AL

Where the Task actually happens. Start typing and Apple Maps suggests matching places, or type the address yourself and skip the search entirely.

InfiniTask never asks for your location. The search is what you typed, nothing more.

Website

WEBSITESRemove
Tickets and travelnationalrail.co.uk
+Add Website

Keep the link with the Task instead of hunting for it again later. Paste a URL and your phone reads the page title once, so the link arrives properly labelled rather than as a wall of address.

Type your own title instead and no request is made at all.

Timeframe

TIMEFRAMERemove
Start1 Jul 2026
End14 Jul 2026

Some work is a stretch rather than a deadline. Give a Task a start and an end and it reads as a run of days, not one date you are already behind on.

Useful for the things that are genuinely in progress rather than simply late.

Reminder

REMINDERRemove
Alert23 Jun 202608:30

A nudge at a time you choose, kept separate from the Due Date. Something can be due Friday and worth remembering on Wednesday.

Notifications are scheduled locally on your device, so nothing about the Task has to leave your phone to make one happen.

Timer

TIMERRemove
1 minute

Work on one Task at a time. Set a length, press play, and the Focus Timer takes over with the rest of the app out of the way.

Start it from the Task, or from the play button on any Task card.

Checklist

CHECKLISTRemove
✓Measure the alcove
✓Order the brackets
Borrow a spirit level
+Add Item

Break a Task into steps and tick them off as you go, without turning one job into five separate Tasks.

Best for the Tasks that are really a short sequence: pack, book, confirm.

Link Note

LINKED NOTESRemove
Kitchen shelvesMeasurements, two photos, the receipt

Some Tasks need more room than a Task gives them. Link one to a Note and the writing, images, checklists and files live in your Notebook, a tap away from the Task.

The Task stays short. The thinking goes where there is space for it.

Importance

IMPORTANCERemove
LowMediumHigh

Low, Medium or High, each with a colour of its own. The Tasks that matter look like they matter, from across the screen, without opening anything.

Leave it off entirely and the Task carries no flag at all.
